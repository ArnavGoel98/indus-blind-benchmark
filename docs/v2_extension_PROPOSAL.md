# PROPOSAL: next pre-registered extension (option A approved 2026-11-18; declared as entry 14)

Written 2026-11-17. **Option A approved by the project owner on 2026-11-18 and declared as pre-registration entry 14; options B and C are not approved.** On approval,
the chosen option becomes pre-registration entry 14, committed before any of its corpora is generated.

## Why another step

The confirmatory run (entry 12) met eight of nine readings. The one it missed, H3, is the result
closest to the Indus question: on syllabic and logo-syllabic scripts, a real relative gave measurable
but small help in 3 of 8 cells. Entry 13 traced this to the syllable-cutter fix, but entry 13 is post
hoc: it was designed after the result was seen, on the same corpora. The explanation "real relatives
help a little on syllabic scripts once their syllables are cut correctly" has therefore been
observed once and never predicted.

## Option A (recommended): a prospective test of the H3 explanation

- **Corpora.** Fresh seeds 12-17 (never generated by any code; checked before declaration), samplers A
  and B, Old Tamil and Finnish, syllabic and logo-syllabic scripts: 48 corpora.
- **Code.** `frozen-v2.1`, unchanged. Real relatives (Malayalam, Kannada, Estonian) with the entry-11
  cutter; C1 unrelated references and C2 floors as in entry 12. No synthetic tiers.
- **Readings, to be fixed in the entry.**
  - X1. Each of the three cells that helped in the final run (Malayalam syllabic, Estonian syllabic,
    Estonian logo-syllabic) helps again by the C1 rule. Met if at least 2 of 3 do.
  - X2. Kannada syllabic and logo-syllabic do not help (both did not, on both seed sets).
  - X3. Every cell in the test stays below 15% mean recovery.
- **What would count against the explanation:** X1 not met (the help does not recur on fresh corpora,
  so the final-run result was noise on top of the fix).
- **Cost.** About 2-3 hours of computing; reported the same day, failures first.

## Option B: separate the relative from its text (design only)

Relationship and text are confounded: every relative is one text of one genre and period (limitation
2 of the v2 section). In the Ugaritic anchor, a genre-matched Hebrew reference added 5-16 points. A
second text for one relative (for example a second Estonian text, or prose Malayalam) would show how
much of each score belongs to the text. This needs new sources with clear licences, a new
normalisation check and the owner's approval of each source, so it is proposed for planning only.

## Option C: Pali

Sanskrit is the only hidden language without a real relative. This waits on SuttaCentral's
permission (`docs/v2_pali_permission_request.md`, not yet sent).

## Not proposed

Changing the solver, its budget or the scoring. Any of these would start a v3 with a new freeze, not
an extension of v2.
